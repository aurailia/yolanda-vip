The five rules that matter this month

✦Respond, don't initiate›

Your energy is built to answer, not to chase. In practice: drop the kit, ask the question, comment in the group — then respond to the real moms who show up. Cold outreach and forced content calendars will always end in frustration for you. Put prompts out into the world, and let your growth come from what answers back.

✦Your gut is the CEO›

Every decision this month gets said out loud as a yes/no question, and the sound that comes back wins — even over your smartest plan. Uh-huh means go. Uh-uh means no, even when the idea looks perfect on paper. Nothing there means not yet, and not-yet is a complete answer.

✦Everything is version one›

Your 3 line learns by trying, not by theorizing. The lead magnet doesn't need to be perfect — it needs to be in front of moms so it can teach you what works. What looks like a mistake is your profile collecting data. Ship it, watch what happens, adjust. That's not failing, that's the method.

✦Let them find you lit›

Cross of Contagion means you don't grow by convincing — you grow because moms catch what you have. A voice note recorded on a good day will outperform a polished post made on a flat one, every time. Protecting your lit-up state isn't self-indulgence. It's your entire marketing plan.

✦Guide when invited›

Your 5 line draws projection: moms will decide you have their answer before they've even met you. Let them come close first — comments, questions, the free layer of the village — and save the deep guidance for the ones who ask. That keeps the projection working for you instead of on you, and it keeps you out of the over-promising trap.

Your daily decision protocol

Small decisions: Say it out loud as a yes/no question — “Do I want to write this post today?” — and follow the first sound, not the second thought. If nothing comes, it's a not-now, and not-now counts.

Big decisions: Don't decide alone in your head — your sacral needs something to respond to. Voxer me and I'll ask you yes/no questions until the answer comes out of your body instead of your mind. Then sleep on it once and ask again in the morning. Two gut yeses make a real yes.
